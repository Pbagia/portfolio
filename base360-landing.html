iv.

Questions, answered.

Which channels does base360 connect?

Instagram and TikTok comments and DMs, Messenger, WhatsApp, SMS, phone calls, and email — all in a single inbox. You reply from base360 without logging into any social platform.

What can the AI agents do?

Reply to comments, answer customers, qualify leads, follow up, and even place calls with an AI voice. You decide which tasks each agent handles and when a human steps in.

How do leads reach my sales team?

Every conversation becomes a lead. High-intent ones are surfaced automatically and handed straight to sales, so nothing slips through and every opportunity gets worked.

Can I run marketing from base360 too?

Yes. Capture subscribers from your website, design and send newsletters and campaigns, and track sign-ups, opens and clicks — all tied to the same customer record.
