About

Hi, I'm Prina.

I'm a product and UX/UI designer with over five years of experience designing complex enterprise applications across healthcare, insurance, and internal operations. I specialize in turning operational complexity into clean, intuitive products that real people genuinely enjoy using.

I've led design on large-scale SaaS platforms — building design systems from scratch, mapping end-to-end user flows, and collaborating directly with product teams, engineers, and stakeholders to bring ambitious products to life. Whether it's a claims intake workflow, an adjuster dashboard, or an AI-powered search experience, I bring the same approach: research-driven, systems-minded, and always focused on the user.

I partner with companies on product design — as a consultant on focused engagements, or as a full-time member of a team building something ambitious.
