Customer Application

A long survey, made linear.

The application carries a long medical survey — deliberately, to remove the in-person medical exam from the underwriting process. The pain point was the sidebar: it let customers jump around, leave sections half-finished, and lose track of where they were. I restructured it as a linear flow with explicit section states, so customers always know what's done, what's left, and what they're in the middle of.

Before

A non-linear sidebar

  • Customers could skip ahead and leave earlier sections unfinished
  • Easy to miss medical-survey questions
  • Time-consuming to jump back and forth to confirm progress
  • Hard to tell which section was actually in progress
After

A linear, stateful sidebar

  • Each section is complete, declined or in progress
  • Customers can't move forward until the current section is done
  • Less time auditing answers, fewer missed questions
  • Clear sense of how much of the survey is left
Health survey — the linear sidebar in action, with Complete / In progress / Declined states for every section.

The Dashboards

Two dashboards, two audiences.

FOXOLIFE runs on two distinct dashboards — one for customers managing their own policy and Longevity Report, and one for the admin team working behind the scenes. Each one carries the same data model, but the surface area, density and priorities are completely different.

Goal

Design two dashboards that share a data model but serve very different jobs — calm clarity for the customer, dense access for the admin.

Customer-facing

Their policy, their report, their kit.

A focused space for the policyholder — track the status of their FOXO kit, view their policy, and unlock their Longevity Report once their epigenetic results are in.

  • Overview — the landing view with To-dos and a quick read on Quote, Application, Policy and Longevity Report.
  • Profile — basic contact information for the policyholder.
  • Insurance — individual policy info, with coverage and cost shaped by the Longevity Report.
  • Kit Status — real-time tracking from ordered through shipped, received and processed.
  • Longevity Report — gated until the epigenetic testing kit is returned and results are in.
  • My Data — additional information on the epigenetic data and how it's used.
Admin-facing

The operational view of every customer.

A working dashboard for the admin team — metrics and to-dos up top, then deep access into every customer's orders, applications, policies and cases.

  • Overall dashboard — high-level metrics and a daily to-do list.
  • Customers — every customer with kit and Longevity Report status; History sits inside each customer record as a full interaction catalogue.
  • Orders — order information and statuses across the pipeline.
  • Applications — application statuses at a glance.
  • Policies — all policy information, indexed by customer.
  • Cases — special circumstances and any issues to resolve through the process.
