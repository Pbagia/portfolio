Color

An ivory base with crisp white surfaces keeps everything soft and pretty, while warm plum replaces harsh black for text. Blush drops back to a gentle tint, and rose is the one confident pop — used for brand moments, momentum, and highlights, always paired with plum for anything you need to read.

Spacing & shape

An 8-point rhythm and soft, generous corners keep everything feeling gentle. Cards round at 24px, inner controls at 14px, and anything tappable becomes a full pill. Shadows stay low and blush-tinted — a whisper of depth, never a thud.
