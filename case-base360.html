01
The problem

B2C brands are buried in messages from everywhere — and no one has a single view of the customer.

Instagram and TikTok comments and DMs, WhatsApp, SMS, calls, email — plus newsletters and marketing in yet another set of tools. Comments go unanswered, leads slip through, replies come late, and the context that would close a sale is scattered across a dozen apps. base360 pulls it all into one warm inbox — answered, qualified, and connected to one customer record.

Unified inbox AI agents Sales CRM Marketing B2C brands
02
What it does

Four things, one connected system.

Every message becomes a conversation, every conversation a lead, and every customer a single record — so social and sales finally live in the same place.

1
One unified inbox
Every social comment (Meta, TikTok), DMs, WhatsApp, SMS, phone calls and email in one place. Never miss a comment again — and reply without logging into a single social platform.
2
AI agents that do the work
Build AI agents for any task — replying to comments, answering customers, qualifying leads, following up, even calling with an AI voice. Your team stops doing the repetitive work.
3
A built-in CRM for sales
Every conversation becomes a lead. High-intent leads are surfaced and handed straight to sales, so nothing gets missed and every opportunity gets worked.
4
Marketing & subscribers
Capture subscribers from your website, design and send newsletters and email campaigns, and track it all — sign-ups, opens, clicks — tied to the same customer record.
